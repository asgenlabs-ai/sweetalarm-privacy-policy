Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 13, 2026

Sweet Alarm ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, and protects your information.

Information We Collect

Sweet Alarm does not collect personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Contacts
  • Photos
  • Messages

The app only stores alarm settings locally on your device.

Advertising

Sweet Alarm uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect anonymous identifiers and device information to provide personalized or non-personalized ads according to Google's Privacy Policy.

Permissions

The app may request permissions including:

  • Notifications
  • Alarm & Reminder Scheduling
  • Vibration
  • Foreground Service (for alarms)
  • Exact Alarm Permission (Android 12+)

These permissions are only used for alarm functionality.

Third Party Services

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob

Children's Privacy

Our application is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Security

We do not store your personal information on any server. All alarm settings remain on your device.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page.
