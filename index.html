Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Sweet Alarm uses Android permissions, device capabilities, locally stored alarm information, advertising services and third-party technologies when you use the application.

Last updated: August 8, 2026
Privacy Summary

Sweet Alarm is an Android alarm-clock application developed by ASGENLABS. Core alarm information is primarily handled locally on the user's device. Sweet Alarm also uses third-party services such as Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads to display advertisements. Those services may automatically process certain information as described in this policy.

1. About This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the Sweet Alarm Android application, package name com.asgenlabs.sweetalarm, developed and published by ASGENLABS.

This policy explains the Android permissions and device capabilities used by Sweet Alarm, how alarm-related information is handled, how advertising services may process information, and what privacy controls may be available to users.

2. Core Alarm Information

Sweet Alarm may store information required to provide alarm and time-management functionality locally on your device.

This information may include:

  • Alarm time and schedule
  • Repeat preferences
  • Alarm labels
  • Alarm sounds and volume-related preferences
  • Vibration preferences
  • Alarm stop-method settings
  • Theme and interface preferences
  • Timer, stopwatch and other application preferences

Sweet Alarm does not require users to create an ASGENLABS account for normal alarm-clock functionality.

3. Android Permissions and Capabilities

Sweet Alarm requests Android permissions and system capabilities that support reliable alarm delivery and presentation.

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Notifications

Sweet Alarm uses notification permission on supported Android versions to display alarm-related notifications and foreground-service notifications.

Android may ask you to grant notification permission. You can manage this permission through your device settings.

android.permission.VIBRATE

Vibration

Sweet Alarm uses device vibration to provide physical alert feedback when an alarm is active and vibration is enabled.

This permission does not provide access to your contacts, messages, camera, microphone, photographs or files.

android.permission.SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM

Schedule Exact Alarms

Sweet Alarm uses Android's exact-alarm capability so eligible alarms can trigger at the specific time selected by the user.

Exact scheduling is important for an alarm-clock application because ordinary background scheduling may otherwise be delayed by Android power-management behavior.

android.permission.USE_EXACT_ALARM

Use Exact Alarms

Sweet Alarm declares this capability because accurately timed alarms are a core function of the application.

android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

Restore Alarms After Device Restart

Sweet Alarm may receive supported Android boot-completed events so scheduled alarms can be restored or rescheduled when necessary after the device restarts.

android.permission.WAKE_LOCK

Wake Lock

Sweet Alarm may temporarily keep the device processor active while an alarm is being delivered or handled.

This helps reduce the chance that Android power-management behavior interrupts a time-sensitive alarm operation.

android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE

Foreground Service

Sweet Alarm may run an Android foreground service while an alarm is actively operating.

This supports user-visible alarm functionality such as sound, vibration and active alarm processing even when the application's normal interface is not in the foreground.

android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK

Foreground Media Playback

Sweet Alarm uses the media-playback foreground-service capability to support alarm audio playback while an alarm is active.

This capability relates to playing alarm audio. It does not itself provide Sweet Alarm with microphone-recording permission.

android.permission.USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT

Full-Screen Alarm Presentation

Sweet Alarm may use Android's full-screen intent functionality to present an active alarm prominently when appropriate, including while the device is locked or another application is visible, subject to Android platform rules and device settings.

Sweet Alarm uses this capability for time-sensitive alarm experiences, not for unrelated promotional full-screen content.

4. Internet and Network Connectivity

Sweet Alarm may use Internet connectivity when a feature or integrated third-party service requires network communication.

Internet connectivity itself does not provide Sweet Alarm with access to your camera, microphone, contacts or personal files.

Sweet Alarm uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertisements.

Google Mobile Ads is a third-party advertising service provided by Google. According to Google's published Mobile Ads data-disclosure information, the SDK may automatically collect and share certain data for advertising, analytics and fraud-prevention purposes.

This may include:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the general location of a device.
  • User-product interactions, such as app launches, taps and video views.
  • Diagnostic information, including information related to application or SDK performance.
  • Device and account identifiers, which may include Android advertising ID, app-set ID or other applicable identifiers.

The precise data processed may depend on the Google Mobile Ads SDK version, application configuration, advertising settings, user privacy choices, region and applicable law.

ASGENLABS does not independently sell personal information collected through Sweet Alarm.
