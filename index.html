Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Sweet Alarm uses Android permissions, device capabilities, locally stored alarm information, advertising services and third-party technologies when you use the application.

Last updated: August 8, 2026
Privacy Summary

Sweet Alarm is an Android alarm-clock application developed by ASGENLABS. Core alarm information is primarily handled on the device. Sweet Alarm also uses third-party services such as Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads to display advertisements. Those third-party services may automatically process certain information as described below.

1. About This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the Sweet Alarm Android application, package name com.asgenlabs.sweetalarm, developed and published by ASGENLABS.

The purpose of this policy is to clearly explain what device permissions Sweet Alarm uses, why those permissions are necessary, how information may be processed, and what choices are available to users.

2. Core Alarm Information

Sweet Alarm may store information required to provide its alarm and time-management functionality on your device.

This may include information such as:

  • Alarm time
  • Repeat schedule
  • Alarm label
  • Alarm sound preferences
  • Vibration preferences
  • Alarm stop-method settings
  • Theme or interface preferences
  • Timer, stopwatch or other app preferences

Sweet Alarm does not require users to create an ASGENLABS account for normal alarm-clock functionality.

3. Android Permissions Used by Sweet Alarm

Sweet Alarm uses Android system permissions and capabilities necessary to provide reliable alarm behavior.

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Notifications

Sweet Alarm uses notification permission on supported Android versions to show alarm-related notifications and foreground-service notifications.

Users can control notification permission through Android system settings.

android.permission.VIBRATE

Vibration

Sweet Alarm uses vibration to provide physical alert feedback when an alarm is active and vibration is enabled.

This permission does not provide access to personal files, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or messages.

android.permission.SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM

Schedule Exact Alarms

Sweet Alarm uses Android's exact-alarm capability to schedule eligible alarms for the precise time selected by the user.

Exact timing is important for alarm-clock functionality because normal background scheduling can otherwise be delayed by Android power and background-management systems.

android.permission.USE_EXACT_ALARM

Use Exact Alarms

Sweet Alarm declares this capability because accurately timed alarms are a core purpose of the application.

android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

Restore Alarms After Restart

Sweet Alarm can receive supported Android boot-completed events so alarms can be restored or rescheduled after a device restart where necessary.

android.permission.WAKE_LOCK

Wake Lock

Sweet Alarm may temporarily keep the device processor active while an alarm is being triggered or handled.

This helps prevent Android power-management behavior from interrupting a time-sensitive alarm operation.

android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE

Foreground Service

Sweet Alarm may run an Android foreground service while an alarm is actively ringing or being processed.

This helps maintain alarm sound, vibration and other active alarm functionality while the app's main screen is not in the foreground.

android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK

Media Playback Foreground Service

Sweet Alarm uses this Android capability to support alarm audio playback through a foreground service.

This capability relates to audio playback and does not itself provide microphone-recording access.

android.permission.USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT

Full-Screen Alarm Presentation

Sweet Alarm may use Android's full-screen intent functionality so an active alarm can be presented prominently when appropriate, including while the device is locked or another application is displayed, subject to Android system rules and user settings.

This capability is used for time-sensitive alarm functionality and not for unrelated promotional full-screen content.
